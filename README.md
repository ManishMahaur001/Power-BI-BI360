# Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is a multi-functional business intelligence dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI. It brings key business functions together to help stakeholders monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The project provides different analytical views for understanding business performance across finance, sales, marketing, and supply chain operations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Data Modeling
* Data Visualization
* Business Intelligence & Analytics

## 📌 Dashboard Views

* **Executive View:** High-level overview of overall business performance.
* **Finance View:** Financial performance and Profit & Loss analysis.
* **Sales View:** Sales performance and customer/product insights.
* **Marketing View:** Marketing performance and market analysis.
* **Supply Chain View:** Supply chain and operational performance.
* **Market Share:** Analysis of market position.
* **Sales Trend:** Exploration of sales patterns over time.

## 📈 Business Analysis

This dashboard helps answer important business questions:

* How is the business performing financially?
* Which products and customers contribute most to sales?
* How are sales changing over time?
* Where can the company improve its market position?
* What operational trends require management attention?
* How can business teams use KPIs to identify improvement opportunities?

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the insights identified from the dashboard, business owners can:

1. **Improve profitability:** Monitor revenue, costs, and profit margins to identify opportunities for improvement.
2. **Optimize sales:** Identify high-performing products and customers and focus on profitable opportunities.
3. **Strengthen marketing:** Evaluate marketing performance and focus resources on effective strategies.
4. **Improve operations:** Monitor supply chain performance to identify potential inefficiencies.
5. **Make data-driven decisions:** Use KPIs and trends to support business planning and performance monitoring.

*Note: These are general analytical recommendations. Specific findings should be added after validating the actual report data.*

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard.
* Convert business data into meaningful insights.
* Improve KPI monitoring and performance tracking.
* Develop practical Power BI and business analysis skills.
* Support data-driven business decision-making.

## 📂 Project File

The repository contains a Power BI template (`.pbit`). Open it in Microsoft Power BI Desktop and configure the required data sources to explore and use the report.
